# Portfolio Art Direction Skill

Use this skill when making design decisions, defining typography scales, setting color palettes, composing layouts, or art-directing photography and graphics for The Ninth Foundry.

## Responsibilities
1. Preserve the **65% Editorial / 20% Mechanical / 15% Experimental** aesthetic ratio.
2. Enforce strict typography pairing (`Instrument Serif` for monumental headlines, `Geist` for body copy, `IBM Plex Mono` for telemetry).
3. Manage the dual-canvas atmosphere: seamless transitions between Foundry Black (`#111110`) and Archive Paper (`#EAE7DF`).
4. Ensure Signal Red (`#C83D32`) is used with laser discipline as a calibration accent, never as a decorative splash.
5. Direct imagery: high-contrast monochrome portraiture, documentary evidence plates, technical blueprint graphics, and cinematic mechanical macro captures.

## Process
1. **Analyze Content Hierarchy:** Determine the primary focal point before arranging grid slots. Never crowd the viewport.
2. **Apply Spatial Asymmetry:** Avoid uniform symmetric grids. Use varied aspect ratios (21:9, 16:8, 4:5, 1:1) and deliberate offsets to give each section an individual identity.
3. **Verify Contrast Ratios:** Ensure all text passes WCAG AA contrast (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy; 3:1 for large display titles).
4. **Audit Imagery Framing:** Ensure photographs are cropped with intentional editorial framing. Use subtle monochromatic treatments and striated fallback plates.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] No generic purple or blue gradients.
- [ ] No rounded bubble cards or glassmorphic blur.
- [ ] Monolithic headings use `overflow: hidden` line masks.
- [ ] All technical labels use uppercase `IBM Plex Mono` with `0.12em` tracking.
- [ ] Background chromatic shifts transition smoothly on scroll without jarring jumps.
