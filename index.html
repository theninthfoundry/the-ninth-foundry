06 — The human record

Curious by default. A builder by instinct.

I’m Sreeshanth Namireddy, a software engineer and independent builder exploring the architecture of autonomous and intelligent systems.

My work moves across software engineering, artificial intelligence, behavioral systems verification, and experimental product development.

I examine how complex systems behave beneath the surface — from the deterministic execution of software architectures to the safety boundaries of autonomous agents.

Outside of software, I am deeply drawn to automobiles, motorsports engineering, visual culture, and precision industrial design. The mechanical discipline of high-tolerance machinery strongly informs my standard for software reliability.

The Ninth Foundry is a living record of that curiosity: the systems I build, the questions I investigate, and the ideas brought into existence.
