## 1. The Core Creative Idea

The Ninth Foundry is an independent digital archive documenting the process of becoming an engineer and builder.

It is not an ordinary resume website, nor is it a commercial agency template designed to convert clients with generic sales funnels. It is conceived as an immersive personal exhibition that exists at the intersection of:
- **An Archive:** Where real systems, tested software, and engineering artifacts are documented with museum-grade precision.
- **A Workshop:** Where unfinished experiments, architectural diagrams, and experimental prototypes are examined.
- **A Field Journal:** Where technical curiosities, motorsport kinematics, and design obsessions are recorded.
- **A Human Portrait:** Where the builder's curiosity, values, and approach to craftsmanship are revealed.

## 2. The Tripartite Creative Ratio

The entire website is governed by an exact creative formula:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 65% EDITORIAL & HUMAN                                                      │
│ High-contrast monochrome portraiture · Monumental serif typography        │
│ Authentic biographical narrative · Intellectual curiosity                   │
├──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 20% MECHANICAL & AUTOMOTIVE          │ 15% EXPERIMENTAL TECHNOLOGY          │
│ Motorsports discipline · Kinematics  │ SVG turbulence shaders · Verification │
│ Rolling wheel physics · Precision    │ Autonomous agent safety systems      │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. 65% Editorial & Human
- High-fashion and brutalist editorial publications (e.g. *Apartamento*, *Cereal*, *The Gentlewoman*, Swiss graphic design monographs).
- Generous whitespace, razor-sharp baseline alignment, asymmetric layout rhythms, and unadorned typography.
- Authentic writing that explains *why* something was built, avoiding corporate jargon or buzzwords.

### 2. 20% Mechanical & Automotive
- Formula 1 telemetry, Porsche engineering archives, and vintage mechanical stopwatches.
- The concept of the mechanical wheel as an instrument of progress: angular rotation mathematically coupled to linear translation.
- High-tolerance structural rules, calibration reticles, coordinate readouts, and figure numbering (`Fig. 01 — Subject`).

### 3. 15% Experimental Technology
- Interactive visual shaders, liquid turbulence displacement maps, and real-time cursor coordinate calculations.
- Deep technical integrity: showcasing behavioral verification of AI-generated code, browser agent safety architectures, and geospatial intelligence.

## 3. The Sensory Atmosphere

### The Dual Canvas
The experience intentionally alternates between two complementary visual worlds:
1. **Foundry Black (`#111110`):** Represents the technical workshop, terminal depths, obsidian night, and focused engineering contemplation.
2. **Archive Paper (`#EAE7DF`):** Represents historical physical documentation, print journalism, daylight exhibition galleries, and structured evidence.

The chromatic shift on scroll between sections 01 and 02 is the definitive atmospheric threshold: the visitor physically transitions from the digital console into the living archive.

### Lighting & Contrast
- High contrast without harsh neon glare.
- Pure White (`#F8F7F3`) provides crisp legibility on dark surfaces.
- Charcoal Ink (`#171715`) provides deep letterpress contrast on light surfaces.
- Signal Red (`#C83D32`) is used as a functional calibration marker: laser datums, hub bolts, and milestone indicators.
